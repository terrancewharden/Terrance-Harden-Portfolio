The role I’m built for

Creative direction that reaches a working product.

I’m not presenting myself as a traditional software engineer. I work at the layer where a rough idea becomes a clear, testable experience—then I learn enough technology to control the build and collaborate honestly.

The through-line is not one tool. It is translating intent into systems: deciding what the product should feel like, making the invisible workflow visible, spotting what is wrong, and staying with the idea until someone can use it.
